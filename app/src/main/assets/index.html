Instagram Following Cleanup

Choose accounts to review, then open each profile in Instagram and confirm unfollow yourself. No password or automatic unfollowing.

Enter your own username. No password is requested or stored.

Your username stays on this screen only; it is not uploaded or used to log in.

2. Quick cleanup — live Instagram

Choose a small batch, then open your Following page in Instagram. Unfollow accounts there yourself; this app cannot press Instagram’s Unfollow buttons or read the live list.

3. Optional: analyze exported data

To identify who doesn't follow you back, choose both followers_1.json and following.json from your official Instagram data export. ZIP files are not supported.

Files are processed locally on this phone and are not uploaded. This app cannot read your live Following list from a username.
